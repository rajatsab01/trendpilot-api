My Process as an Analyst for Generating BTCUSD Trades

Fetch Current Price and Recent Data: I pull the live spot price for BTCUSD using financial APIs (e.g., Polygon or CoinGecko proxies). For deeper analysis, I retrieve recent OHLCV (Open-High-Low-Close-Volume) bars—1-minute for scalping (intraday momentum) and 1-hour/daily for long-term (trend structure). This gives me the baseline "current price" and raw material for price action review.
Analyze Market Sentiments: I scan web sources (news, indices like Fear & Greed) and X (Twitter) for real-time buzz. Key queries: "bitcoin market sentiment October 26 2025" and recent posts with operators like "bitcoin OR btc sentiment since:2025-10-25." This reveals crowd psychology—e.g., fear levels, institutional flows (like BlackRock's $324M ETF inflows), or whale activity. Deep Dive into Leading Indicators: Using code execution, I compute metrics like RSI (Relative Strength Index for overbought/oversold), MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence for momentum crossovers), and simple moving averages (e.g., 50/200-period). For scalping, focus on short-term (14-period RSI on 1-min bars); for long-term, longer horizons. Current read: RSI ~35-40 (oversold, signaling potential bounce), MACD histogram contracting negatively but nearing a bullish divergence—suggesting exhaustion in the sell-off.
Price Action Review: I examine candlestick patterns, support/resistance levels, and volume. Recent action: BTC crashed ~$370B market-wide in early October, consolidating around $104K-$110K support. No clear breakout yet, but higher lows forming a potential ascending triangle—bullish if volume picks up.
Trade Synthesis:

Direction: Bullish if indicators/sentiment align for upside (e.g., oversold bounce); bearish if momentum confirms downtrend.
Entry/TP/SL: Entry near current price or key level (e.g., support bounce). TP at next resistance (Fibonacci extensions or prior highs). SL below recent low (1-2% risk for scalping, 5-10% for long-term).
Risk-Reward Ratio (RR): Target 1:2 minimum (risk $1 to make $2+), scaled by volatility.
Success Probability: Estimated via historical backtest analogs (e.g., similar RSI/MACD setups win ~60-70% in bull cycles) + current sentiment weight (e.g., -10% for bearish bias).
Recommendation Percentage: Overall conviction (80-90% for high-alignment setups; lower if conflicting signals). I only recommend if RR >1:1.5 and probability >50%.
